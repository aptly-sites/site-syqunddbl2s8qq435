Application Standards

General Requirements

All applicants must pass through a screening process. Complete every field, using N/A when it does not apply. A completed application is required for each adult (anyone 18 years of age and older) occupying the property. Applications are taken on a first-come, first-served basis.

Income Requirements

Employment must be current and verifiable for a minimum of one (1) year. Other verifiable income equaling three (3) times annual monthly rent. Verifiable income includes as confirmed by employer, trust officer, tax returns, or other satisfactory documentation as required. Each roommate should earn 1 ½ times the rent. If one or more roommate(s) do not, at least one roommate must meet the three (3) times requirement.

Fastest review: link your bank or payroll account through Findigs (via Stripe or Pinwheel).

On average these applications are reviewed same-day.

You may also upload PDF income documents if preferred, though this may cause delays in your application processing.

Credit and Rental History

To determine satisfactory credit worthiness, we run a credit report soft credit check. The minimum credit score is 605. Applicants below this may still qualify with a guarantor or added deposit. No open bankruptcies will be considered.

Must have a minimum of one year of verifiable positive rental/residence history and no negative rental history for the previous five years.

Prior or pending eviction

Foreclosures within the past three (3) years with unresolved judgment

Monies owed to an apartment community or landlord

Bankruptcy in the last six (6) years or pending Bankruptcy

Falsifying information on application

If an applicant fails to meet any of the above criteria, he/she may be asked to pay additional security deposit up to, but not exceeding 1 ½ times the rent and/or given the option of obtaining a co-signer (who must also meet above criteria.)

ID Verification

All applicants must verify identity through Findigs using a valid Government ID. Military IDs are not accepted through the system – contact us if needed.

Criminal History

Not only must Applicant have acceptable credit history, additionally, all Applicants must meet our Criminal History Criteria.

Applicants that are registered sex offenders will be denied. Applicants must have no felony convictions less than 10 years old that involve violent crimes against persons or property, including but not limited to murder, arson, kidnapping, assault, bomb related offenses, robbery or burglary, terrorism OR that involve the manufacturing or distribution of drugs in any manner. All other felony convictions must be more than 5 years old. Conviction of any drug related offenses involving possession only, or alcohol related offenses where no one was permanently injured or killed, must be at least 2 years old. Successful completion of any felony sentence at least 2 years ago and no new criminal activity for at least 2 years before this application is also required. No Applicant with any outstanding warrants or crime that is awaiting trial will be accepted. If the Applicant would like management to review additional information regarding the felony conviction or the current arrest or warrant as part of their rental application, the Applicant is permitted to submit that information to management along with their application and Management will review that information on a case by case basis.

Animals

All animals, including assistive animals, must be disclosed. We ask that all residents specify their animal status using Findigs Pet Screening. This allows residents to easily add animals to their lease or indicate that there are no animals in their unit. Whether you have a pet, assistance animal, or are submitting an animal visitation request or animal complaint, everything will be done through Findigs

To add an animal to your lease, please upload the following before your move-in or lease renewal date:

A picture of your animal

A picture/copy of an up-to-date rabies vaccination record

Documentation from a health professional for assistance animals

Pet Deposit and Fees

There is a $300 pet deposit per pet.

There is a $25 monthly pet rent per pet.

PET RESTRICTIONS

  • There is a maximum of 2 pets allowed per unit.
  • Male cats are not allowed.
  • Female cats must be spayed and declawed, or spayed and outdoor only.
  • If any of the following breeds are kept as Pets, it is required that the tenant has their own animal liability insurance covering the specific dog and naming The Pruitt Team’s brokerage and the property owner as additional insured on the policy: Pit Bull Terriers, Staffordshire Terriers, Rottweilers, German Shepherds, Presa Canarios, Chows, Doberman Pinschers, Akitas, Wolf-hybrids, Mastiffs, Cane Corsos, Great Danes, Alaskan Malamutes, Siberian Huskies.
  • For standard household pets, there is an annual charge of $35 for the first pet, and $20 for any subsequent pets, paid to Findigs.
  • All animals must be approved by the Owner regardless of Findigs Screening and carry a specific liability policy naming the owner and West USA Realty as added insured parties.

*THIS CRITERIA DOES NOT APPLY TO ANY ASSISTIVE ANIMAL.

Tenant agrees that the animal(s) will be licensed in accordance with all applicable governmental provisions. Owner/Landlord and/or Management reserve the right to require Tenant to immediately remove the animal(s) at any time upon any violation of Tenant of these rules.
